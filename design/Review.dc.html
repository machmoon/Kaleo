4 findings

Every connection was checked against the pin definitions in the manufacturer's datasheet, not just against the netlist. Each finding cites the page it came from.

will not work U1 · pin 44
BOOT0 is floating
Nothing is connected to pin 44. On the STM32F030 this pin is sampled at every reset to choose the boot source, and a floating input will latch randomly — the board will boot to the bootloader on some power-ups and to your firmware on others. Tie it to GND through a 10 kΩ resistor.
STM32F030C8.pdf · p.31, §5.3.2
will not work U3 · C4
Output capacitor is the wrong dielectric
C4 is a 10µF ceramic. The AMS1117 needs at least 22µF of tantalum on its output; a ceramic's ESR is far below the 0.3–22 Ω the control loop needs, so the regulator will oscillate. C3 on the input is already 22µF, which suggests the two got swapped.
AMS1117.pdf · p.9, Table 2
marginal U1 · C7
Decoupling capacitor is 8.4 mm from the pin it bypasses
C7 decouples VDD on pin 48, but the placer put it across the board. At that distance the loop inductance is roughly 12 nH, which makes the capacitor useless above about 4 MHz — well below the 48 MHz core clock it is meant to be filtering.
AN2586 · p.14, §3.1
sourcing U2
DRV8838 is not stocked in this package
The WSON-8 variant shows zero stock at your two configured suppliers, with a 22-week lead time. The DRV8837 is pin-compatible, in stock, and rated to 1.8 A — matching what your motor supply already assumes.
