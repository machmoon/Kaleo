why is C4 flagged?
The AMS1117 needs a tantalum output cap. You have a 10µF ceramic, and ceramic ESR is too low for the regulator's control loop — it will oscillate.
datasheet p.9
COUT ≥ 22µF tantalum
ESR 0.3–22 Ω for stability
C3 on the input is already 22µF, so this is likely just a mix-up between the two.
